What won

Flows. Automation revenue tripled to ₹74,884 once post-purchase attribution came online. Checkout recovery alone earned ₹45,873 at 23× ROI, and Post-delivery returned ₹18,376 on ₹68 of spend.

What improved

Order quality. AOV rose 46% to ₹854 and returning customers now make up 34% of orders, up from 24%. The store is selling less often to better customers.

What to watch

Traffic. Sessions fell 38% and new orders 43%. Retention is doing more with less, but the top of funnel needs acquisition support or the returning pool stops refilling.

Where October goes

Diwali month, and one urgent fix.

Fix delivery first

Delivery fell from 86.7% to 66.7% as volume doubled. Some sends landed below 20%. Cut send volume, drop unengaged numbers, and check the WhatsApp quality rating before Diwali.

Lean on VIP

Third month running that a VIP send topped the table, now at 45.5×. Bought 3+ is up 13.9% to 7,893. Run early-access and last-call around every Diwali drop.

Shift budget into flows

Checkout earned ₹1,41,965 from 1,031 entries. Flows now make 42% of CRM revenue with no list cost. Build Diwali-specific cart and browse flows as in August.

The tester pool, again

10,198 people and flat for three months. It was flagged in August and hasn't moved. It needs a full-size conversion offer, not another tester.
