How I take it further — July plan (Month 2)

Complete the journey, bring email fully online, and wake the WooCommerce list. Flows keep doing the heavy lifting; campaigns stay light.

Finish post-purchase — add SMS + RCS

Layer SMS/RCS onto confirm/ship/deliver, split above vs below ₹500 AOV, and close out revenue attribution with KwikEngage.

Browse abandonment — split by crowd

₹1-tester viewers → cheap RCS/SMS flow; everyone else → WhatsApp 3-touch, personalised by scent viewed.

Welcome flow — Meta audience

Popup + welcome for paid/intended traffic, route men vs women, capture scent preference, plug in the scent-finder quiz.

Replenishment — per SKU

Same-scent refill timed to run-out for full-size buyers; tester→full-size nudge for the ₹1 crowd. Separate men & women.

WooCommerce list activation

Wake the dormant legacy list with the ₹1 tester + top-5 singles, on separate flows so ROI is cleanly attributable.

Email fully online + light July campaigns

Complete the 30-email warm-up to unlock email as a 9–15× channel; run ~1–2 WhatsApp + 1 email/week toward repeat rate & CLV.
